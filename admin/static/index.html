Players

Everyone signs in once on the games page and gets 2 chances across all games. Starting the 2nd chance drops the 1st result, so the prize shown here is what they take home. A player is done once they took their prize or started their 2nd chance; after that their Instagram and name cannot sign in again. Allow again forgets the player and their results (for a typo or a crash), so they can sign in fresh.

Leaderboard open

Each player's best result per game, one row per Instagram account (Instagram usernames are only shown here, never on the public leaderboard). Delete removes all of that player's results in that game (use it for rude or fake names). Reset clears every leaderboard, for example before the next event day.
